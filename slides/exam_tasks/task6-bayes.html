Bayes: MLE asks "which θ fits the data?" I also ask what we believed before the data. Prior → likelihood → posterior.

Hogg 11.2.2 · Book Unit 4.3. Your prior $\mathrm{Gamma}(35,51)$; $\bar x=90.37$.

$f(\theta\mid x)\propto f(x\mid\theta)\,f(\theta)$

Read the Gamma carefully

Data: Gamma with $\alpha=3$, $\beta=1/\theta$ → scale $1/\theta$, so $\theta$ is a rate:

$f(x\mid\theta)=\dfrac{\theta^3}{\Gamma(3)}x^2 e^{-\theta x}$

Prior $\theta\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(35,51)$ — Hogg convention: β is also a rate (matches published 11.2.2 solutions).

Do not mix scale and rate. Wrong convention → wrong posterior.

Posterior: just add the counts

Likelihood $\propto\theta^{30}e^{-\theta\sum x_i}$. Prior $\propto\theta^{34}e^{-51\theta}$.

$\theta\mid x\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(65,\,954.7)$ (shape–rate)
because $35+30=65$ and $51+10\cdot 90.37=954.7$

Point estimates: mean vs mode
