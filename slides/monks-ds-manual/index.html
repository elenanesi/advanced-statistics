MONKS DS FIELD MANUAL

Penny: Each card is what a client or AM actually asks. Then plain English of what that means. Then which concept to open — not jargon first.

Six shelves: Did the activity work? · Who is valuable? · Weird counts · Timing & fatigue · Model trust · Sharpen the brief.

How to read a card

“They said X — what do they want?”
Plain English: the decision they’re trying to make.
Reach for: the stats / method idea → where to study it
Fork that saves weeks: users were randomly split into A vs B → experiment / A/B toolkit. Everyone saw the same promo / site change → causal impact (no clean control). Don’t paste a p-value on a payday spike.

1 · Did the activity work?

Media, promo, site, creative — “was it worth it?”

“Revenue jumped during the sale — was that us, or payday / season?”
They want incrementality: what would have happened without the campaign.
Reach for: Causal impact / BSTS counterfactual → causal-impact skill
“We redesigned checkout permanently — did growth rate change, or only a one-day blip?”
Permanent change → level + slope story. Short promo → total £ lifted over the burst.
Reach for: ITS regression (β₂, β₃) vs BSTS cumulative lift → AGENTS.md decision rule
“We A/B tested two landing pages — is B really better, or luck?”
Randomised traffic; decide if the difference is big enough to trust.
Reach for: Hypothesis testing (Type I / II, power) → exam task4 · Unit 7
“Which creative wins on click-through / purchase rate?”
Each person either clicks (or buys) or doesn’t — a yes/no outcome. You’re comparing rates between variants.
Reach for: Bernoulli / binomial · A/B on rates → exam task1 · Unit 3.1
“Did average order value go up?” (but the analyst runs a two-sided test)
The business only cares about increase. Testing “any change” answers a different question and wastes power.
Reach for: One-sided vs two-sided hypotheses → exam task4

2 · Who is valuable / what should we bid?

VBB, LTV, targeting, media efficiency.

“Google asks for a value per click — what number should we send?” (e.g. Wickes VBB)
Predict expected order value / LTV so bidding follows profit, not just conversions.
Reach for: Regression + calibration · bias–variance → wickes-vbb · Unit 6
“The value model uses geo, device, season, creative… coefficients go crazy.”
Too many noisy knobs; the fit chases noise and won’t hold next week.
Reach for: Ridge / regularization → exam task5
“Don’t just give me 47.2 — how sure are we?”
They need a range / probability, not a single heroic point estimate.
Reach for: Bayesian posterior · credible intervals → exam task6 · CausalPy
“Who is likely to buy (or churn) so we can suppress / upsell?”
Score each person with a probability between 0 and 1; act on the high/low scores.
Reach for: Propensity / logistic (Bernoulli cousin) → Unit 3.1 + applied ML

3 · Weird counts & volatile KPIs

Orders, sessions, tickets — integers that misbehave.

“Daily orders: average is 15 but some days are 2 and some are 60 — alerts keep firing.”
Variance is bigger than a simple Poisson allows (“overdispersion”).
Reach for: Negative binomial / Gamma–Poisson → ch3-distributions
“How many support tickets / stockouts should we expect this afternoon?”
Counting rare-ish events in a time window; sometimes “time until next one.”
Reach for: Poisson · Exponential waiting times → Unit 3.3
“New market / new SKU — almost no history. What’s a sane rate to plan with?”
Blend a prior belief (category average) with the tiny new sample.
Reach for: Conjugate Gamma / Bayesian rate update → exam task6
“AOV / revenue per user jumped because three whales checked out.”
The mean is dominated by a few extremes; the “average customer” story is lying.
Reach for: Heavy tails · robust summaries / log / mixtures → Unit 3 practice

4 · Timing, journeys, fatigue

CRM, lifecycle, creative wear-out.

“After signup / click, how long until first purchase?”
Not if they convert — when. Timing drives nurture cadence and budget pacing.
Reach for: Survival / waiting-time distributions → exam task2
“Half convert in 2 days, half take months — one average is useless.”
Two (or more) behaviours mixed in one KPI; one curve hides the split.
Reach for: Mixture models → exam task2
“The launch week looked great — did the effect die by week three?”
They want the daily effect over time, not one total that hides fade-out.
Reach for: Time-varying effect α(t) in BSTS → causal-impact
“Same ad kept running — are people getting bored / more likely to ignore?”
Risk of response changes with exposure length (creative fatigue).
Reach for: Weibull hazard regimes → ch3-distributions

5 · Can we trust the model?

Readouts that survive a sceptical client.

“The curve fits last month perfectly and dies next week.”
Overfitting: model memorised noise. Need smaller / shrunk coefficients.
Reach for: OLS vs ridge · bias–variance → exam task5
“The model says θ̂ = 4.13 — treat that as exact?”
A point estimate still has sampling noise; show uncertainty or a check.
Reach for: MLE uncertainty · Normal / bootstrap → Unit 6.1 + 3.2
“Two systems / hops in a funnel — what’s the total wait / capacity?”
Combine component distributions into one system number (sum of random times).
Reach for: Sums of Gammas / Erlang → exam task3
“Last quarter’s baseline + this campaign’s new data — update the plan.”
Prior belief revised by evidence → a new posterior you can act on.
Reach for: Bayes update · conjugate priors → exam task6

6 · Sharpen the brief

Before modelling — fix the question.

“Prove impact” — but there’s no holdout geo and no A/B.
Observational design; pick a quasi-experiment, don’t fake an RCT.
Reach for: ITS / DiD / synthetic control taxonomy → causal-inference
“We tested 20 creatives / 12 regions — three look significant!”
Many shots at the same goal inflate false wins.
Reach for: Multiple testing / false discovery → Unit 7.3
“Optimise for clicks” when the business cares about profit.
The loss function (what you punish/reward) must match the decision.
Reach for: Loss functions (mean vs mode vs £) → task6 mean-vs-mode
“Media and creative keep arguing past each other in the readout.”
Agree the estimand first: £ lift? daily effect? P(positive)?
Reach for: Name the estimand out loud → marketing-ds-solutions

Quick chooser

They say…You open…
“Did the campaign cause this?”Causal impact / ITS
“Which page / creative wins?”A/B · Bernoulli · hypothesis test
“What value do we bid?”Regression + ridge · VBB
“How sure are we?”Bayes / intervals
“When do they convert?”Survival / mixtures
“Model looks insane next week”Regularization · bias–variance
